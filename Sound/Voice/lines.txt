# Orbiter's crew voice (F1: the onboard voice), one line per XRSound default sound that is
# speech: "<XRSound file name>|<text to say>". In-game, with "Callout voice: Orbiter", a request
# for XRSound/Default/<name>.wav plays Sound/Voice/<name>.flac instead (System::FindSound).
# The text is what XRSound's recording says (checked with Whisper), in one style; numbers spelled
# out; acronyms spelled the way that makes the voice say the letters (chosen by ear: A.P.U.,
# R.C.S., A-F, P-S-I, O2, delta-V). Not listed: XRSound's non-speech sounds, tones and music,
# and "ambl" (a slogan, left as XRSound's).
# Made by Utils/Audio/make_voice.py --engine qwen3 --voice crew (kept takes: lines.takes.json).
# Docking: XRSound's latch sound, then the line (a "bed": "|||<sound>@<s>").
