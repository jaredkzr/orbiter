# Mission Control's replies (Src/Orbiter/Audio/Radio.cpp): "<Event>_<voice>_<n>|<text>|<voice>".
# Each event has three lines, each said by both CAPCOMs (Utils/Audio/voices: capcom1 "c1", capcom2
# "c2"). One CAPCOM is on shift at a time (8-hour shifts by the simulation's clock, alternating): the
# radio plays one of that CAPCOM's takes at random, never the same one twice running, a pause after the
# crew's callout (and the light's round trip). Generic on purpose: no call sign, no quotes from real
# missions. Made by Utils/Audio/make_voice.py --engine qwen3 --lines Sound/Voice/Ground/lines.txt
# --max-rise -1.0 --end-stop -24 (the ending falls a semitone or more; capcom2 ends firmly); kept takes:
# lines.takes.json, the reworked ones chosen by ear from --candidates. Takes that say "Houston" are
# played at the Earth and the Moon only (Radio::SaysHouston); each event has three that don't.
